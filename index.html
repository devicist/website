About

Devicist is the studio of Nick Stedman, an artist, creative technologist and university lecturer with over 20 years of experience building embodied experiences with electronics, coding, and fabrication. His award-winning robotic artworks have been showcased globally, including at Ars Electronica, the Ontario Science Center, SIGGRAPH and on Japanese TV. Since founding Devicist in 2014, Nick and his team have partnered with fabricators, agencies, architects, and creators on diverse projects, from permanent installations to trade show centerpieces.
